# Changelog

## 1.1.0
- Find other shops now opens a map inside the app. Parts store and other links open in the app's in-app browser panel instead of leaving the app.
- How-to videos for each repair now play inside the app (needs the optional video key on the server). Without it, a YouTube search link is shown.

## 1.0.0
- First version: vehicle profile, photo or typed quote check, line-by-line price ranges for 42 common repairs, state labor rates, luxury, SUV and EV handling, mileage checks, fee checks, parts links, do-it-yourself ratings, in-app guide, quote reader server.

## 1.1.1
- Built-in server option so photo reading, videos and the map work for everyone with no key.
- Daily caps per phone and across all users; friendly message when a cap is reached.
- Settings server fields are now an optional advanced override.

## 1.1.2
- Shop finder opens on the shops closest to you, with a search-area slider, a city/ZIP box and a Near me button.
- How-to videos only appear when the server has video search turned on, so nothing sends people to YouTube by surprise.
- Location permission added through the Android build.

## 1.1.3
- Electric and hybrid cars are recognized from make and model (Tesla, Leaf, Bolt, Mach-E, Ioniq, ID.4, Prius and more) and the Fuel setting switches by itself.
- Electric cars no longer show engine jobs (oil, spark plugs, alternator and so on) in repair lists or to the quote reader.
- New electric-only checks: battery coolant, reduction gear fluid, high-voltage battery health check, charge port repair.
- Electric-only services on a gas car are flagged, and engine services on an electric car are flagged.

## 1.1.4
- Parts list inside the app: photo, price, shipping, condition and where the seller ships from, filtered for your vehicle. Appears when the server has parts search turned on.

## 1.1.5
- The parts button always shows. Until live listings are switched on it shows the typical parts cost for your vehicle and the store links.
